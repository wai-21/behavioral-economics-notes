# ⚠️ Methodology Boundary & Known Blind Spots

> This page is not a disclaimer — it is **part of the archive's credibility**. Anything about "can I act on this" starts here.

### 1. The fundamental source limitation (most important)

| Limitation | Detail | Consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The raw material is a dialogue, not the book** | All "what the book says" blocks are reconstructed from an AI tutoring conversation over nine chapters. **The book itself was not compared page by page.** | Chapter structure and concept attribution are trustworthy (cross-checked against third-party bibliographic data), but **any challenge of the form "that's not what the book actually says" cannot be answered** |
| **The AI paraphrase layer** | The "golden quotes" are mostly AI paraphrases of the book's positions, not verbatim text | All tagged **confidence: medium** and never treated as quotable original text |
| **Concept attributions were added by me** | Names like Simon / Kahneman / Thaler / Keynes / Loewenstein / Klein / Duflo were **not in the source material** | Added after separate verification, mostly **medium** confidence (Simon, Kahneman, Tversky, Thaler, Keynes rated **high**); see [Appendix C](C-bibliography-verification.md) |

### 2. Three collaboration incidents the material itself exposed (kept verbatim)

| Type | Incident | Cost | My countermeasure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Language switching** | When I answered wrongly in topic 04, the correction arrived in English (*"That is not quite it."*) | The correction was right, but I paid an extra round just to re-read my own error | Language consistency is now part of the output spec; on any state switch, re-align language first |
| **State contamination** | I replied "1" to pick a strategy; it was read as a math question about the number 1 | A wasted round; I had to pull it back with "Chinese plz" | Single-character answers are dangerously ambiguous; important choices should repeat the option name |
| **Answer leakage** | The Kenya fertilizer-voucher question's correct answer was exposed by a citation tag | That answer is **excluded from mastery assessment** | Any answer "found near the question" is marked as not independently solved |

### 3. Sample and method limits

- **n = 1**: every "how it rewired me" comes from **a single dialogue chain**, unverified against a second person, a second explanation, or empirical data. **A different guide might have produced different conclusions.**
- **Guidance effect (cannot be excluded):** every "insight" happened after an AI question. I cannot prove the conclusions were mine rather than led — **this archive should also be read as a record of being guided**.
- **The wargame's persuasiveness bias:** the scenarios (toast, daycare, popcorn) persuade through **vividness**, not representativeness. The more vivid the example, the more its generality gets overestimated — availability heuristic, showing up inside the very notes about it.
- **Self-assessment bias:** the roadmap's "current status" labels ("internalized," etc.) are self-applied and **externally unverified**. They measure what I *think* I've done.

### 4. Appendix A's rules: how far they can be trusted

- **Never backtested:** all 24 rules come from **logic derivation + mechanism mapping**; none has been backtested or validated out-of-sample. Their value is "constraining behavior," not "predicting returns."
- **Not investment advice:** a personal decision-training record. Any real trading decision based on them belongs entirely to the decision-maker.
- **What the confidence tags mean:** the source's "high/medium" measures **theoretical solidity of the mechanism** — high confidence ≠ high returns ≠ high win rate.
- **Conflict priority undefined:** e.g. rule 4 (buy the capitulation bottom) and rule 6 (volatility spike → no operations) can fire simultaneously in extreme sessions; **the source gives no adjudication order.** A known gap to close before live use.

### 5. Explicit coverage gaps

| Gap | Status |
| :--- | :--- |
| Academic portions of the book (public-policy nudge debates, neuroeconomics fMRI) | **Actively filtered** by the source; not covered |
| The **mathematical form** of the probability weighting function (Prelec function, parameters) | Only the "inverse-S curve + over/under-weighting directions" level; no formal model |
| Prospect theory value-function **parameters** (λ, α, γ) | Only the magnitude "loss ≈ 2–2.5× gain" |
| Term-by-term verification of **Chinese translations** of terms | Body is Simplified Chinese; terms follow the source's usage; not checked against the Yilin edition's official translations |
| **Backtest and performance data** for the 24 rules | Entirely missing |

### 6. The correction promise

If any item above is overturned by new evidence (the book's actual text, backtest data, official translations), **this archive changes directly and does not defend its old conclusions**. Its value is not in being right now — it is in recording a reasoning process that can be corrected.
